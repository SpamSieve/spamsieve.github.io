How It Works

Step 1: Install & Connect

Download and link SpamSieve to your email client. It scans incoming mail instantly using pre-trained Bayesian models.

Step 2: Train & Adapt

Review and label messages—SpamSieve learns your patterns. Whitelists protect known senders; blocklists catch repeat offenders.

Step 3: Filter Everywhere

Colors flag spam levels. Train from any device; rules apply across accounts. Privacy assured—nothing leaves your Mac.

Step 4: Monitor & Refine

View logs and stats for precision. Customize notifications and rules for perfect control.
